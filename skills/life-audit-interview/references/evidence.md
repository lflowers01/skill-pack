# Evidence and honest limits

Bottom line: the pieces with the best evidence are if-then plans, specific goals with feedback, and a check-in with accountability. Everything else is borrowed mechanics. Expect a nudge, not a transformation.

## Origin and what it is not
- Tarantino described a "Quentin detest fest" on Joe Rogan Experience #2240 (with Roger Avary): stay up all night listing what he was doing wrong, spend the last two hours on how to change it, then move neighborhoods and change peers. About 1.5 years later he was earning as a screenwriter.
- It is one uncorroborated recollection of events about 35 years old. The transcript has no speaker labels, so attribution is inferred.
- "Night test" is not his term and appears in no source found. Do not use it.
- Buffett's 25-5 list is folklore. Inc. traces it to a Scott Dinsmore anecdote; Buffett reportedly said he could not recall making a list (secondhand). The technique is usable; the attribution is not.
- Founder rituals (Gates Think Week, Ferriss fear-setting, Sivers /now, Bezos regret minimization, Dan Koe's reset) are survivor self-reports with no causal evidence. Dan Koe's is known only through a third-party summary.
- No study tests an AI life-audit interview directly.

## Design decision
Copy the structure (long honest diagnosis, short design phase, change of environment and peers), not the all-nighter. Sleep loss raises emotional reactivity and often worsens risky choices (small lab studies; Ben-Simon 2015, N=18; a 25-study scoping review with inconsistent results). The original design proposed four rested sittings over about ten days and roughly 4.5 hours total. The sitting lengths and 11 pm cutoff are design judgments, not research findings; use flexible timing.

## Evidence ranking

| Tool | Evidence | Use |
|---|---|---|
| If-then plans (implementation intentions) | d = 0.65 over 94 studies (Gollwitzer and Sheeran 2006), likely inflated by publication bias | Every commitment ends as an if-then rule |
| WOOP / mental contrasting + if-then | g = 0.34 raw, 0.24 after bias adjustment, 21 studies (Wang 2021); live delivery 0.465 vs documents 0.277; shrinks at scale (Kizilcec and Cohen) | Run conversationally per chosen goal, after an expectancy check |
| Specific goals with feedback | .42 to .80, weaker on complex tasks (Locke and Latham 2002) | Dated targets, a check-in, learning goals for novel work |
| Autonomous vs pressured motives | Controlled motives showed no link to progress; correlational, very heterogeneous (Seger 2025) | Ask the four "why" flavors per goal; probe goals held from guilt |
| Motivational interviewing style | Skilled open questions and reflections drive change talk (r = .55, Magill 2018); small effects vs active comparators | Behavioral floor: open questions, reflections, no unasked advice |
| Solution-focused questions | 74% of 43 controlled studies positive, weak comparators (Gingerich and Peterson 2013) | Scaling and exceptions questions |
| Best Possible Self | d about .33 to .51 on mood and optimism, not behavior (Carrillo 2019) | Orientation only, always followed by an obstacle step |
| Pre-mortem | Reduced overconfidence but did not raise action (non-peer-reviewed poster) | Use it, then force "which reasons are in my control" |
| Designing Your Life, fear-setting, Wheel of Life, Harada, Koe | No controlled outcome studies (DYL's only study is n=8 qualitative) | Borrow mechanics (energy log, three alternative lives, prototypes), claim nothing |

## Cautions that shape the design
- **Never end on the vision.** Positive fantasizing without obstacles predicted worse outcomes in several studies (Oettingen).
- **Check expectancy first.** Mental contrasting helps when expected success is high; with low expectation people let go. "I don't think this will work" is information.
- **Startup ideas** (Paul Graham, YC Startup School; practitioner opinion): notice real problems rather than brainstorm, get to the leading edge as a builder, log gaps without judging, try to sell before building. "AI" or "robotics" is a toolset, not a problem.
- **AI coaching:** the one preregistered RCT (N=517, Claude-based career coach, arXiv 2603.17887, abstract-level read, two-week follow-up) found goal progress d=0.33 vs no support, but no difference from a matched written questionnaire. The AI's added effect ran through perceived accountability. So the commitments and check-in step is the likely active ingredient.
- **Sycophancy is the central risk.** Models gave emotional validation in 76% of responses vs 22% for humans (ELEPHANT, not peer reviewed). Users rate sycophantic answers higher. Models struggle to recover once a sycophantic trajectory is set. Use structural checkpoints, not a "be honest" line.
- **Time-box.** Heavy voluntary chatbot use correlated with worse psychosocial outcomes (MIT/OpenAI, N=981, correlational). End with offline actions.
- **Restate safety.** Multi-turn crisis handling is weaker than single-turn (developer's own evals).
- **Facilitate, do not interrogate.** Google "Substance over Style" (arXiv 2503.19328, 31 users): facilitative flow preferred, interrogative least preferred, excessive questioning reduced engagement.
