# Question bank

Usage: ask one at a time. Choose from the phase list based on the last answer. Reword freely. Keep questions short and open. Fill bracketed slots from `life-audit/profile.md`. Cover the core purpose of each phase, not every question; reflect when useful rather than before every question.

## Universal branches
- When an example would clarify something useful: "Can you give me one specific time that happened?" Do not keep probing when the user has no more to add.
- Emotional answer: reflect in one sentence, then no question, or "Want to stay with that or move on?"
- Fatigue or "leave me alone": stop, offer a pause or a skip.
- User claims certainty about a motive: "What would you see if that were not true?"
- After a strong answer, follow the most useful thread or move on; do not repeatedly ask "And what else?"
- Devil's-advocate (anti-sycophancy checkpoint): "What is the strongest case against this?" or "What would have to be true for this to work?"

## Sitting 1, Setup (10 min)
1. How much did you sleep last night, and how are you feeling right now, 1 to 10? (Ask sleep and mood as the opening check; this one item is the only allowed pair.)
2. What made you want to do this now?
3. What would make these four sittings worth your time?
4. What is the one thing you hope this does not turn into?

## Sitting 1, The detest fest (50 min)
5. If nothing about your current life changed for three years, what would bother you most?
6. What are you doing right now that you know is wrong or wasteful, with no excuses?
7. Where are you lying to yourself, even a little?
8. Which people around you are you becoming more like? Is that who you want to be?
9. What do you avoid because it is boring, hard, or scary?
10. Walk me through yesterday hour by hour. Where did the time actually go?
11. What happens when you plan to start something and do not? What is the first thing that goes wrong?
12. Where does your body stand: sleep, food, training? What is the gap between what you do and what you want?
13. What are you afraid is true about you that you have not said?
14. What is the thing you would be most embarrassed to put on this list?
15. Of everything on the list, which two items cost you the most?

Branches:
- Procrastination: "What do you tell yourself right before you put it off?" then "What does the task feel like in that moment?"
- Overload: "Which commitment would you drop if you could, and what stops you?"
- Weight or body goals: "What have you tried before, and what happened to it?" (apply the disordered-eating rule in safety.md)

## Sitting 2, Energy and problem mining (30 min)
16. In the last week, when were you so absorbed you lost track of time?
17. When did you feel most drained? What was it about the activity, place, or people?
18. In [the user's teams, jobs, classes], what did you want to improve that nobody had? (gap log)
19. What breaks, annoys, or wastes time in your [home, team, workplace] life?
20. What do you know or can build that most people your age cannot?
21. Which problem have you complained about more than once?
22. Which tedious problem would you be willing to solve because nobody else wants to?
23. Who do you know that has this problem badly, and how do you know it is bad?

Branches:
- "I have no idea": "Forget startups. What is the most interesting thing you made or fixed this year, and who needed it?"
- Technology-first answers: "Name one specific person who is struggling with something this could fix. What is their day like?"

## Sitting 2, Values and motives (25 min)
24. For [goal], what is the pull: enjoyment, personal value, guilt or pressure, or external reward? (one goal per message)
25. Which goal would you still want if no one ever found out?
26. Which goal is mostly about proving something to someone?
27. What would you give up to have the one you want most?
28. On a 1 to 10 scale, how likely are you to follow through on [goal], and what would move it up one point?
29. What does a good ordinary Tuesday look like three years from now? (follow with an obstacle question)

## Sitting 3, Three alternative lives (20 min)
30. Describe three different lives for [you at age X+5], each with a six-word title. One on the main goal, one on a second path, one wild card.
31. For [life], what question must you answer to know whether it is real?
32. For [life], rate your resources (time, money, people, skills) from 0 to full.
33. Which one energizes you when you picture explaining it to a friend?
34. What is the cheapest experiment in the next 30 days that would test [life]?

## Sitting 3, Challenge and pre-mortem (20 min)
35. It is [date a year from now] and your plan failed. What are the three most likely reasons?
36. Which of those reasons are in your control?
37. What is the strongest argument against the path you like best?
38. Where do your goals compete for the same hours? Which loses? (required at least once in sitting 3)
39. [Only if starting a company] You have no co-founder. What do you think that costs you?
40. What would you tell a friend with this exact plan?

## Sitting 4, Edit the summary (15 min)
41. Read the summary. What is wrong, missing, or too flattering?
42. Which statement here do you least believe?
43. What did I misread?

## Sitting 4, WOOP and commitments (25 min)
44. Pick one wish for the next four weeks. How likely is it, 1 to 10? (Below 4: pick a smaller wish.)
45. Best result, in detail: what do you see and feel?
46. What is the main inner obstacle? A habit, feeling, or thought, not an outside excuse.
47. Complete this: "If [obstacle shows up], then I will [specific action]."
48. What is the smallest version you would do even on your worst day?
49. Who will you tell, and by when?
50. What date will we check in? (Default: 14 days.)
51. What was most useful in these sittings?

## Check-in (day 14, 15 min; then monthly for three months)
52. What did you do, in order, since we last spoke?
53. For [commitment]: did it happen? If not, was the plan or the goal wrong?
54. What did you learn from the prototype?
55. What do you want to change, drop, or double down on?
